## Brand & Style

This design system translates the intimacy of a bespoke Parisian couture salon and high-fashion editorial folios into a digital interface. Designed for luxury fashion discovery, custom wardrobe composition, and private styling appointments, it replaces software sterile conventions with tactile warmth, deliberate quietude, and print-grade typographic hierarchy.

The aesthetic fuses **Haute Couture Minimalism** with **Warm Bespoke Tactility**:
- **Quiet Luxury Sensibility**: Digital clutter is pared down in favor of generous margins, delicate hairlines, and intentional breathing room that frames garment craftsmanship.
- **Warm Atelier Canvas**: Cold digital slates and blue-tinted whites are banished. The ground is defined by luminous porcelain and warm alabaster silk papers accented with rich bordeaux velvet and champagne foil warmth.
- **Editorial Poise**: High-contrast serifs, subtle micro-tracking, and bespoke material callouts emulate limited-edition monographic lookbooks.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from atelier materials: structured wools, deep wine velvets, bespoke gilt stamping, and handmade warm rag papers.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#4A1521`) & Tertiary (`#3B121B`)**: Bordeaux Velvet and Bordeaux Noir anchor primary actions, active navigation anchors, and signature couture marks. They represent focused luxury intent without resorting to sterile digital blacks.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880`)**: Warm Champagne Gold serves as a muted metallic accent for atelier seals, monogrammed badges, and subtle highlighted states. Never use it as large background fills; reserve it for precision strokes, icons, and micro-embellishments.
- **Canvas & Neutral Surfaces**: The primary background canvas is `canvas-silk` (`#FAF8F5`). Layered panels, lookbook card backings, and drawer sheets use `surface-alabaster` (`#F5F2EC`), while garment image viewports rest within pristine `surface-porcelain` (`#FFFFFF`) to ensure uncompromised textile color fidelity.
- **Ink & Typography**: Editorial text is anchored in Deep Obsidian Espresso (`#141212`) for high-contrast clarity. Secondary text, technical garment attributes, and styling captions rely on muted taupe-bronze (`#706963`). Micro-metadata and hairline borders utilize `#9C948D` and `#E8E3DC`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes haute couture editorial display styling with crystalline, contemporary legibility.

- **Editorial Display & Headlines**: **Playfair Display** introduces authentic fashion-editorial presence. Reserved for editorial features, look titles, silhouette descriptions, and section breaks. Keep letter-spacing slightly condensed on large display headings (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to echo high-end editorial mastheads.
- **Functional Atelier Sans**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** powers navigation, garment technical sheets, pricing tables, styling metadata, and inputs. It provides neutral, precise contrast against the expressive serif headlines.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy**: All label roles (`label-lg`, `label-md`, `label-sm`) feature uppercase formatting with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) to replicate engraved atelier tags and garment care seals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces the spatial architecture of high-fashion print monographs: generous outer margins, unhurried focal breaks, and disciplined columnar alignment.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (375px - 480px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1.25rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Full-width styling canvases and single-column garment cards ensure tactile clarity.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Introduces paired lookbook spreads, juxtaposing full-length photography with fabric detail macros.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout bound to a maximum width of `1320px`, using `margin-desktop: 3rem` and `gutter-desktop: 2rem`. Editorial content and apparel galleries alternate across asymmetrical columns (e.g., 7-column hero imagery against 5-column curation essays).

### Rhythmic Discipline
- Spacing inside elements prioritizes comfortable luxury framing (`space-md` to `space-lg`).
- Editorial sections are separated by expansive vertical gaps (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`), enforcing an intentional, relaxed cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial depth relies on tonal layering, delicate structural hairlines, and ambient warm-tinted illumination rather than artificial dark drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - Ground: `canvas-silk` (`#FAF8F5`).
  - Elevated Mounts: Cards and editorial panels use `surface-alabaster` (`#F5F2EC`) or pure `surface-porcelain` (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Separation: Achieved through crisp `1px` structural hairline borders in `hairline-stone` (`#E8E3DC`) or translucent bordeaux (`hairline-bordeaux`).
- **Atelier Ambient Light**: Floating controls, lookbook drawers, and active popovers float with an ultra-diffused bordeaux-tinted shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(59, 18, 27, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(20, 18, 18, 0.03)`
- **Atmospheric Scrim**: Overlays for private fitting bookings and wardrobe drawers utilize a frosted alabaster veil: `rgba(245, 242, 236, 0.82)` coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

## Shapes

The shape architecture is architectural, disciplined, and bespoke. The default roundedness is soft (`1`), prioritizing tailored geometry over overly casual rounded forms.

- **Lookbook Mounts & Product Frames**: Sharp-to-soft corners (`0.25rem` / `4px` to `0.5rem` / `8px`) that mirror gallery pass-partouts and archival mat boards.
- **Interactive Action Points & Badges**: Retain an exception of full pill profiles (`roundedness: 3` / `9999px`) exclusively for primary call-to-actions, category swatches, and bespoke status tags to ensure pleasant haptic tap targets.
- **Drawers & Overlay Sheets**: Restrained `0.75rem` (`12px`) top radii to maintain a sleek architectural presence.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Atelier Button**: Solid `bordeaux-velvet` (`#4A1521`) fill, pure white label in `label-lg` with uppercase tracking. Pill-shaped (`9999px`), minimum height `48px`, horizontal padding `space-lg`. On hover or press, transitions deeply into `bordeaux-noir` (`#3B121B`) with an ambient warm glow.
- **Secondary (Bespoke Outline)**: Transparent background, `1px` crisp border in `hairline-stone` (`#E8E3DC`), label in `ink-obsidian` (`#141212`). On press, shifts to `surface-alabaster` with a hairline stroke in `bordeaux-velvet`.
- **Atelier Accent Button**: Deep bordeaux background with a fine `1px` metallic border in `champagne-gold` (`#C5A880`) for private client services and VIP reservations.
- **Text Action**: Pure text in `bordeaux-velvet`, styled in `label-md` with an active hairline underline displaced by `4px`.

### Chips & Selection Filters
- **Filter Pills**: Height `34px`, pill profile. Inactive state: `surface-alabaster` with `1px solid #E8E3DC`, text in `ink-taupe` (`#706963`).
- **Active State**: Solid `bordeaux-velvet` with white text, or pure `surface-porcelain` bordered in `1.5px solid #4A1521` with `ink-obsidian` text.
- **Haute Couture Badges**: Petite capsules in `champagne-light` (`#F3EDE4`) with text in `champagne-gold` (`#C5A880`) or `bordeaux-velvet` for silk blend, archival edition, or made-to-measure designations.

### Cards & Lookbook Viewports
- **Lookbook Display Card**: Grounded in pure `surface-porcelain` (`#FFFFFF`), bounded by `1px solid #E8E3DC`. Images honor a disciplined 3:4 or 4:5 portrait ratio with generous internal margins so silhouettes breathe without touching edges.
- **Curated Silhouette Capsule**: Minimalist presentation featuring silhouette contours, subtle muted-taupe metadata, and an unobtrusive champagne gold monogram accent.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Input Fields**: Architectural containers with subtle `0.25rem` corners, background in `surface-porcelain` or `surface-alabaster`, enclosed by `1px solid #E8E3DC`. Input text in `ink-obsidian`, placeholder in `ink-muted`.
- **Focused State**: Shifts border to `1px solid #4A1521` with an ultra-soft hairline focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(74, 21, 33, 0.08)`.

### Selection Controls
- **Radio & Checkboxes**: Custom circular controls featuring a `1px` perimeter in `hairline-stone`. Selected state: rich `bordeaux-velvet` core framed by an interior porcelain gap.
- **Textile & Swatch Selectors**: Exact fabric textures displayed within circular tokens, bordered by a subtle concentric whitespace halo and a delicate `#4A1521` selection perimeter.

### Editorial Additions
- **Garment Provenance & Fabric Notes**: A dedicated warm alabaster card with a subtle champagne-gold left keyline (`2px solid #C5A880`), displaying mill origin, textile composition, and weave weight in `body-sm`.
- **Capsule Palette Strip**: A discrete horizontal micro-scale revealing the color distribution of a curated ensemble (e.g., 65% Alabaster, 25% Bordeaux Noir, 10% Champagne Gold).
